Release-specific rule
---------------------

The license delivered with a particular copy controls that copy. Commercial
terms do not retroactively relicense an earlier MIT or AGPL copy. ChordVox
v1.5.132 and later may be delivered as commercial ChordVox Pro only when the
artifact includes the matching commercial license and notices.

Security boundary
-----------------

Production bundling, minification, ASAR integrity, code signing, and License
Key checks raise the cost of tampering and prevent accidental shipment of raw
source files. They do not make client-side code secret or impossible to reverse
engineer. The customer device is treated as untrusted: server private keys,
license peppers, administrative tokens, payment secrets, and shared backend
credentials must remain on controlled server infrastructure.
